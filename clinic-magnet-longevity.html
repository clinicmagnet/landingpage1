What is a longevity assessment?+

Longevity assessments are health evaluations focused on increasing the odds of you living a longer, fuller and healthier life. These might include and combine medical history evaluations, blood tests, various imaging sessions, fitness and body composition measures, and a medical and clinician consultation. The exact scope varies by provider.

Why compare providers in Bangkok?+

Bangkok offers a wide range of reputable, highly rated private hospitals and specialist clinics within a short flight from Singapore. Comparing helps you understand the differences before committing to a visit.

Does Clinic Magnet recommend one specific clinic?+

We organise potential options around the preferences you share. Clinic Magnet strives to be neutral and focused on your needs. We generally offer 3 different options for consideration, though sometimes, based on your unique needs, we might recommend less.

Is this medical advice?+

No. The comparison is general information and does not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional.

What happens when I tap the WhatsApp button?+

WhatsApp opens with a ready-to-edit enquiry. Sending it starts a conversation about the kind of comparison you are looking for, without any obligation to book.
